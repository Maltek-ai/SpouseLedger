## Section 14: First 30 Days Checklist  _(sharing: now)_
_Work through this in order. Check each box as you go. Ask the attorney before acting on anything legal or financial._

- **Checklist: First few days**
  - Lean on the people in Section 1 and Section 12. Accept help.
  - Contact the funeral home or arrangements contact. Ask them how many certified death certificates to order; many offices need originals.
  - Call the attorney (Section 1) and find the will / trust (Section 8).
  - Call the CPA and bookkeeper. Review cash and the next 30 days of payroll and bills.
  - Call the insurance agent. Ask about life, key-person, health continuation, and anything with a claim deadline.
  - Locate the sealed emergency envelope (Section 13) and get into the password manager.
  - Tell key employees and crew leads calmly: jobs and paychecks will continue. Keep it short, and don't promise anything long-term.
- **Checklist: First two weeks**
  - File life insurance claims. Ask each carrier what they need.
  - Notify Social Security (the funeral home often does this) and any pension or annuity providers.
  - Contact the mortgage servicer and other lenders. Ask about next steps and don't skip payments.
  - Visit the banks with death certificates. Ask what is frozen and what is available (Section 6).
  - Make sure autopay is working for essential bills: utilities, mortgage, insurance, payroll, taxes.
  - Review Section 5 with the attorney before any decision on the business.
- **Checklist: First 30 days**
  - Notify the three credit bureaus to flag the account and prevent identity theft.
  - Update titles, beneficiaries, and account ownership with the attorney's guidance.
  - Handle phones, email, and subscriptions. Close or take over accounts after confirming nothing is needed (Section 13).
  - Tell the school, the pediatrician, and the childcare providers what has changed.
  - Schedule time with the CPA to cover quarterly tax payments and payroll tax filings.
  - Make a plan for the business with the attorney and the person in Section 4: keep, sell, or wind down.
  - Give yourself permission to rest. Nothing here has to be perfect.
